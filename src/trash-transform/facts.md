# trash-transform 知识断言

## 适用范围

- 覆盖小学低年级对常见可回收材料的分类，以及塑料、金属、纸张、玻璃和厨余的典型再生去向。
- 使用理想化、定性的回收路径帮助建立“按材料分拣”意识；不覆盖具体回收率、地区分类标准差异、复合包装拆解工艺或工业能耗数据。

## 知识断言

- [F1] 可回收物需要按材料种类分拣；不同材料进入不同的回收与再加工流程。[S1]
- [F2] 常见 PET 塑料瓶可被回收再加工成聚酯纤维，用于纺织品等产品。[S2]
- [F3] 铝制饮料罐可反复回收熔炼，再制成新的铝制品。[S3]
- [F4] 废纸可经碎浆、除杂等工序再造成新的纸制品。[S4]
- [F5] 废玻璃可被清洗、粉碎并熔融后制成新的玻璃容器或其他玻璃产品。[S5]
- [F6] 果皮、菜叶等厨余有机物可通过堆肥分解，转化为可改善土壤的有机质。[S6]

## 来源

- [S1] U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, “How Do I Recycle? Common Recyclables”: materials should be sorted by type for recycling systems. https://www.epa.gov/recycle/how-do-i-recycle-common-recyclables
- [S2] Association of Plastic Recyclers / industry education on PET bottle-to-fiber recycling pathways; PET bottles are commonly reprocessed into polyester fiber. https://plasticsrecycling.org/
- [S3] The Aluminum Association, “Recycling”: aluminum cans are recyclable and can be remelted into new aluminum products. https://www.aluminum.org/Recycling
- [S4] U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, “Paper and Paperboard: Material-Specific Data”: recovered paper is used to manufacture new paper products. https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling/paper-and-paperboard-material-specific-data
- [S5] Glass Packaging Institute, “Recycling”: cullet from recycled glass is melted to make new glass containers. https://www.gpi.org/recycling
- [S6] U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, “Composting At Home”: food scraps can be composted into a soil-improving material. https://www.epa.gov/recycle/composting-home
